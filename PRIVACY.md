# Aiki Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-22_

You can use Aiki anonymously — no account is required.

If you create an account, we store your email, password hash, and
preferences (such as your operating hours, session duration, reward time,
and the websites you have designated as distractions or as the place you
would rather be) on servers we operate. We do not share this data with
third parties.

## Contact
